# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in VedaX welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and focused on the work. Give and receive feedback professionally. Respect privacy and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, malicious disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.
